## Notes from the Original Author

- Some parts of the planets remain to be mapped. These "gaps" are filled with fictional terrain that corresponds with the rest of the landscape.
- The colors are slightly more saturated in order to highlight a unique nature for each object.
- Earth textures are the most precise part of the pack — a result of merging and adjusting large amounts of geo-data, space photos, and images from NASA's Blue Marble.
- True Earth colors (as seen from space) may vary depending on the camera used, duration of exposure, lighting conditions, and atmospheric conditions.
- Seasonal variations affect Earth's flora and snow coverage. Earth in these textures has a lot of vegetation and less snow.
